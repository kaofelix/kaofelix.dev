I went through places like [Liveheats](https://www.liveheats.com/), [Sahaj](https://sahaj.ai/), [Typeform](https://www.typeform.com/), and [ThoughtWorks](https://www.thoughtworks.com/). A lot of my approach was shaped by principles of [Extreme Programming](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/ExtremeProgramming.html) and I've been practising [Test Driven Development](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/TestDrivenDevelopment.html) and chasing true agility since 2010.

[Emacs](https://www.gnu.org/software/emacs/) used to be the center of my coding universe, but these days I mostly build software in conversation with the [Pi](https://pi.dev/) coding agent inside [Herdr](https://herdr.dev/). I like to tinker with [my setup](https://github.com/kaofelix/dotfiles) a lot. Building my own tools to try and understand what agents are doing, and make the process work for me.

I like making useful things for myself, but often just being a fun idea is reason enough, so I tend to follow my curiosity.
